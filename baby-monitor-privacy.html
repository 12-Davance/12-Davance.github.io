Privacy Policy — Baby Monitor

Package ID: com.personal.babymonitor · Developer: [dvd/tech] (Dawit Derebie) · Effective Date: November 18, 2026

Baby Monitor ("the App") is developed and published by [dvd/tech]. We respect your privacy and designed the App so that all live video camera streaming, audio monitoring, and parent-to-baby communication take place directly between your own devices over your local Wi-Fi or Personal Hotspot network.

1. Camera & Microphone Streaming (`CAMERA` & `RECORD_AUDIO`)

  • How they are used: The App uses your device's camera (`CAMERA`) and microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`) on the Baby Station to stream live video and audio to the paired Parent Station, and uses the microphone on the Parent Station when you hold or toggle the "Push to Talk" button to speak to your baby.
  • 100% Local Network Transmission: Video frames and audio stream directly over a peer-to-peer local socket on your local Wi-Fi router or Personal Hotspot. Video and audio are never uploaded to any cloud server, never recorded to disk, and never shared with the developer or third parties.
  • Foreground Service Notification: Whenever monitoring is active, Android displays a persistent notification so you are always aware that local camera and audio monitoring is running and can stop it at any time.

2. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

To keep the App free, we display a muted banner advertisement using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Google AdMob may automatically collect and process:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID / AD_ID)
  • Coarse IP-based location, device model, OS version, and ad interaction metrics (impressions and clicks)
  • Diagnostic crash/performance data related to ad delivery

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your Android device's Settings → Google → Ads. Learn more about how Google handles data at Google's Privacy & Terms.

3. Data Retention & User Preferences

Your app preferences (such as chosen theme, cry sensitivity threshold, and white noise selection) are stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. Uninstalling the App permanently removes all local settings.

4. Children's Privacy

Baby Monitor is a utility tool intended for use by parents, guardians, and adult caregivers. The App is not directed at children under the age of 13.
