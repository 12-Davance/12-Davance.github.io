Baby Monitor — Privacy Policy

Package ID: com.personal.babymonitor · Effective Date: November 18, 2026
Privacy Summary

Baby Monitor operates directly between your own devices over your local Wi-Fi or Personal Hotspot network. Live camera video and microphone audio are never uploaded to cloud servers, never recorded to disk, and never shared with the developer.

1. Camera & Microphone Streaming (CAMERA & RECORD_AUDIO)

  • How they are used: The application uses your device's camera (CAMERA) and microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) on the Baby Station to stream live video and audio to the paired Parent Station, and uses the microphone on the Parent Station when you hold or toggle the Push-to-Talk button to speak to your baby.
  • 100% Local Network Transmission: Video frames and audio stream directly over a peer-to-peer local socket on your local Wi-Fi router or Personal Hotspot. Video and audio never leave your local network.
  • Foreground Service Notification: Whenever monitoring is active, Android displays a persistent system notification so you are always aware that local camera and audio monitoring is running and can stop the session at any time.

2. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

To support development while keeping the application free, a muted banner advertisement is displayed using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Google AdMob may automatically collect and process:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID / AD_ID)
  • Coarse IP-based location, device model, OS version, and ad interaction metrics (impressions and clicks)
  • Diagnostic crash and performance telemetry related to ad delivery

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your Android device's Settings → Google → Ads. For full details on how Google processes data, refer to Google's Privacy & Terms.

3. Local Storage & User Preferences

Your configuration settings (such as selected theme, microphone sensitivity threshold, and white noise selection) are stored locally on your device via Android SharedPreferences. Uninstalling the application permanently removes all stored preferences.

4. Children's Privacy

Baby Monitor is a household utility designed exclusively for parents, guardians, and adult caregivers. The application is not directed at children under the age of 13.

5. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the application's data practices, contact dvdtech.apps@gmail.com.
