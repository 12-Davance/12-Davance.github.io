Battery Notifier — Privacy Policy

Package ID: com.personal.batterynotifier · Effective Date: November 18, 2026
Privacy Summary

All battery telemetry (percentage, charging current, voltage, and temperature) is read from Android system broadcasts and processed strictly on your device. No personal information or battery usage history is transmitted to external servers.

1. On-Device Battery Telemetry & Spoken Alerts

  • Local Processing: Battery percentage, charging current (mA), temperature (°C), voltage, and health status are read locally via standard Android system broadcasts (ACTION_BATTERY_CHANGED).
  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, transmit, or store your battery logs, personal identity, contacts, or location on any external server.
  • Text-to-Speech (TTS): Spoken voice alerts use Android's built-in on-device Text-to-Speech engine.

2. System Permissions

  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used to keep real-time battery threshold monitoring active while charging and deliver low-battery, charge-target, and overheat alerts.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Used to automatically resume battery monitoring after a device reboot when monitoring is enabled.
  • REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS: Optional user-initiated exemption so system battery savers do not interrupt spoken charge alerts while the screen is off.

3. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

To support development while keeping the application free, a muted banner advertisement is displayed using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Google AdMob may automatically collect and process:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID / AD_ID)
  • Coarse IP-based location, device model, OS version, and ad interaction metrics (impressions and clicks)
  • Diagnostic crash and performance telemetry related to ad delivery

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your Android device's Settings → Google → Ads. For full details on how Google processes data, refer to Google's Privacy & Terms.
