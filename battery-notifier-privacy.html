Privacy Policy — Battery Notifier

Package ID: com.personal.batterynotifier · Developer: [dvd/tech] (Dawit Derebie) · Effective Date: November 18, 2026

Battery Notifier ("the App") is developed and published by [dvd/tech]. The App monitors your device's battery percentage, charging status, voltage, and temperature locally on your device to announce customizable spoken voice and notification alerts.

1. Battery Telemetry & Local Processing

  • 100% On-Device Processing: All battery readings (percentage, charging current in mA, temperature in °C, voltage, and health status) are read from standard Android system broadcasts (ACTION_BATTERY_CHANGED) and processed strictly on your device.
  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, transmit, or store your battery usage history, personal identity, contacts, or location on any external server.
  • Spoken Alerts (TTS): Voice announcements use Android's built-in on-device Text-to-Speech engine.

2. Permissions Used

  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used to keep battery threshold monitoring reliable in the background and deliver low-battery, charge-target, and overheat alerts.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Used to automatically resume battery monitoring after your phone restarts if monitoring was enabled.
  • REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS: Optional user-initiated setting so aggressive OEM battery savers do not kill spoken charge alerts while your screen is off.

3. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

To keep the App free, we display a muted banner advertisement using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Google AdMob may automatically collect and process:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID / AD_ID)
  • Coarse IP-based location, device model, OS version, and ad interaction metrics (impressions and clicks)
  • Diagnostic crash/performance data related to ad delivery

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your Android device's Settings → Google → Ads. Learn more about how Google handles data at Google's Privacy & Terms.
