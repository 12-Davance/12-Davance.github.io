Privacy Policy — Air Hockey

Package ID: com.personal.airhockey · Developer: [dvd/tech] (Dawit Derebie) · Effective Date: November 18, 2026

Air Hockey ("the Game") is developed and published by [dvd/tech]. The Game provides single-player AI matches, two-player pass-and-play on a single screen, and local Wi-Fi / Personal Hotspot multiplayer matches.

1. Local Wi-Fi Multiplayer & Game Data

  • No Accounts Required: You do not need to create an account or sign in to play any mode of the Game.
  • Local Wi-Fi & Hotspot Discovery: When you play a Wi-Fi match, your device communicates puck and mallet coordinates directly with the opponent's device over your local Wi-Fi network or Personal Hotspot using UDP/TCP sockets. No gameplay traffic is routed through external servers.
  • Optional In-App Feedback: If you choose to submit a message using the in-app Feedback form, your message text, optional contact field, and basic device model/OS version are transmitted to the developer solely to respond to your feedback or bug report.

2. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

To keep the Game free, we display a muted banner advertisement using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Google AdMob may automatically collect and process:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID / AD_ID)
  • Coarse IP-based location, device model, OS version, and ad interaction metrics (impressions and clicks)
  • Diagnostic crash/performance data related to ad delivery

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your Android device's Settings → Google → Ads. Learn more about how Google handles data at Google's Privacy & Terms.

3. Local Storage

Game settings (such as AI difficulty, target goals, sound/vibration toggles, and dark/light rink theme) are stored locally on your device via Android SharedPreferences and are deleted automatically if you uninstall the Game.
