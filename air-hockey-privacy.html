Air Hockey — Privacy Policy

Package ID: com.personal.airhockey · Effective Date: November 18, 2026
Privacy Summary

Air Hockey requires no user accounts or sign-ins. Local Wi-Fi and Personal Hotspot multiplayer matches exchange puck and mallet coordinates directly between devices on your local network without routing gameplay through external servers.

1. Gameplay & Local Wi-Fi Multiplayer

  • No Account Required: You can play all single-player, two-player pass-and-play, and local Wi-Fi multiplayer modes without creating an account or providing personal information.
  • Local Network Discovery: During a Wi-Fi match, your device communicates puck and mallet positions directly with your opponent's device over your local Wi-Fi router or Personal Hotspot via local UDP/TCP sockets.
  • Optional In-App Feedback: If you voluntarily submit a message through the in-app Feedback form, your message text, optional contact field, and basic device model/OS version are sent to the developer solely to address your feedback.

2. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

To support development while keeping the game free, a muted banner advertisement is displayed using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Google AdMob may automatically collect and process:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID / AD_ID)
  • Coarse IP-based location, device model, OS version, and ad interaction metrics (impressions and clicks)
  • Diagnostic crash and performance telemetry related to ad delivery

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your Android device's Settings → Google → Ads. For full details on how Google processes data, refer to Google's Privacy & Terms.

3. Local Preferences

Game preferences (AI difficulty, target score, sound and haptic settings, and dark/light table theme) are saved locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences and are removed automatically if the game is uninstalled.
